## Context

The forces at play: what problem, which constraints (platform, CLIs, users,
security), which options were on the table. Facts, not advocacy.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two paragraphs. Name the modules or files that
carry it out.

## Consequences

to keep, follow-up work). Include the costs honestly.
